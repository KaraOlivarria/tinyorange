# Texture — Founding Board Prospectus

## The invitation

Texture is incorporating as a 501(c)(3) to make world-class textbooks a
public good: open-source, openly licensed K-12 textbooks (then higher ed),
free digitally and at-cost in print, built on **txt**, our git-based
collaborative typesetting platform. We are recruiting a founding board of
five directors to govern the organization from incorporation through its
first published book.

## What the board does — and deliberately does not do

Directors carry the standard fiduciary duties (care, loyalty, obedience):
strategy, budget, hiring the Executive Director, legal compliance, and
fundraising leadership. **The board does not control book content** — an
independent Editorial Board does ([governance](../community/governance.md)).
Directors must be comfortable with, and committed to defending, that
firewall; it is the foundation of the organization's credibility.

## Profiles sought (one seat each, roughly)

1. **K-12 education leader** — superintendent, chief academic officer, or
   equivalent; knows how adoption actually works in districts.
2. **Publishing / editorial executive** — has shipped books at scale;
   understands editorial operations, production, and print.
3. **Open-source / technology leader** — has grown an open-source project
   or platform community; understands infrastructure economics.
4. **Nonprofit finance / legal** — CFO, counsel, or foundation officer;
   anchors the audit and compliance function.
5. **Chair** — any of the above with prior board leadership and
   fundraising range.

We actively seek a board that reflects the students the books serve —
in geography, background, and community.

## Expectations

- **Term:** 3 years, staggered; maximum two consecutive terms.
- **Time:** 4–6 board meetings/year (remote-friendly) + one committee
  (Finance & Audit, or Governance); realistic total ≈ 4–6 hrs/month,
  heavier in year one.
- **Fundraising:** a personally meaningful annual gift and active
  door-opening. No fixed give/get in year one; the founding board sets the
  policy it will then live under.
- **Compensation:** none — board service is volunteer (expenses
  reimbursed). Standard for public charities and required for credibility.
- **Conflicts:** annual disclosure under the
  [conflict of interest policy](conflict-of-interest-policy.md); note that
  publishing-industry relationships are specifically covered — they don't
  disqualify, but they are disclosed and managed.
- **Liability:** D&O insurance from day one; indemnification per bylaws.

## Why serve

Founding directors set the constitution of an institution designed to
outlive them: the licensing one-way door, the editorial firewall, the
at-cost print commitment. If Texture succeeds, the marginal cost of a
world-class education's materials goes to approximately zero — and the
founding board is who made the promises that got it there.
