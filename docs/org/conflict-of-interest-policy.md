# Texture — Conflict of Interest Policy (Draft)

> Based on the IRS sample policy (Form 1023 instructions, Appendix A),
> adapted for Texture. Adopt at the organizational board meeting. **Not
> legal advice.**

## 1. Purpose
To protect the corporation's interests when it contemplates a transaction or
arrangement that might benefit the private interest of a director, officer,
or key employee ("covered person"), or that might result in an excess
benefit transaction.

## 2. Definitions
- **Financial interest:** a covered person has a financial interest if they
  have, directly or indirectly, through business, investment, or family: an
  ownership or investment interest in, or a compensation arrangement with,
  any entity with which the corporation has or is negotiating a transaction
  or arrangement.
- Texture-specific note: **publishing-industry relationships count.**
  A covered person employed by, consulting for, or holding equity in a
  commercial educational publisher, print vendor, or edtech company doing
  or seeking business with Texture must disclose it.

## 3. Procedures
1. **Duty to disclose.** A covered person must disclose the existence and
   nature of any actual or possible conflict to the board or relevant
   committee.
2. **Recusal and determination.** The interested person leaves the meeting
   during discussion and vote; remaining directors determine whether a
   conflict exists.
3. **Addressing the conflict.** The board may approve the transaction only
   if, after due diligence (including comparability data where relevant), a
   majority of disinterested directors determines it is in the corporation's
   best interest, for its own benefit, and fair and reasonable.
4. **Violations.** Failure to disclose is grounds for corrective action up
   to removal.

## 4. Records
Minutes must record: names of persons who disclosed, the nature of the
interest, who was present for discussion and vote, and the decision and
rationale, including comparability data relied upon.

## 5. Compensation
No voting member of the board or any committee whose jurisdiction includes
compensation matters may vote on their own compensation.

## 6. Annual Statements
Each covered person annually signs a statement affirming they have received,
read, and understood this policy, agree to comply, and understand that the
corporation must engage primarily in activities accomplishing its exempt
purposes.

## 7. Editorial-Specific Conflicts
Members of the Editorial Board and lead reviewers additionally disclose:
authorship or royalty interests in competing commercial textbooks, paid
advocacy relationships relevant to content areas, and any payment offered by
third parties to influence Texture content. Such payments must be refused
and reported. See [../community/governance.md](../community/governance.md).
