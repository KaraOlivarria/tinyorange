## Open textbooks, written by the world's best educators, free for every student.

**The problem.** U.S. schools and families spend billions annually on
textbooks whose prices have far outpaced inflation, while the books
themselves are closed: errors persist for years, teachers can't legally
adapt materials to their classrooms, and the expertise of thousands of
brilliant educators never compounds. In higher education, textbook cost
directly changes what courses students take and whether they keep the book.

**The insight.** Software had this exact problem — expensive, closed,
slowly improving — until open source and its toolchain (version control,
code review, mass collaboration) made world-class software free. Nobody
has built that toolchain for textbooks. We are.

**What Texture does.**

1. **Publishes open textbooks.** Rigorous, standards-aligned K-12 core
   curriculum first, then high-enrollment college courses. Free digital
   editions forever (CC BY 4.0); print at the lowest sustainable cost —
   a fraction of commercial list prices.
2. **Builds txt**, the open-source collaborative typesetting tool: git-based
   version control for books, structured editorial review, and automated
   professional typesetting to web, PDF, EPUB, and print. Educators
   collaborate through a browser; every change is reviewed by credentialed
   subject-matter and pedagogy experts before it ships.
3. **Runs transparent editorial governance.** An independent Editorial
   Board — not donors, not directors — controls content. Every claim in
   every book is traceable to who reviewed it and when.

**Why this succeeds where OER has struggled.** Open educational resources
exist but remain fragmented: static PDFs, inconsistent quality, no
maintenance path. Texture's difference is *infrastructure*: living books
with a repair loop (a teacher reports an error today; it's fixed in the
next build), professional design from a single source, and a collaboration
platform that lets the best educators work together instead of alone.

**The team & status.** Founding stage: incorporating as a 501(c)(3),
recruiting a founding board of education, publishing, and technology
leaders. The txt specification is complete and public; development begins
immediately upon funding.
