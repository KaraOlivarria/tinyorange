# Texture — Trademark Policy (Draft)

Version 0.1 (draft) — for counsel review alongside trademark registration
([formation checklist, Phase 4](../org/formation-checklist.md)). Modeled on
the Mozilla and Wikimedia trademark policies.

**The marks:** "Texture" (in connection with educational publishing and
software), the "txt" wordmark, the Texture logo, and "Texture Books."

**The principle:** our *content* is open (CC BY 4.0 — copy, adapt, sell,
translate, no permission needed). Our *identity* is not. The marks exist so
that "a Texture book" reliably means "passed Texture's editorial review."
That guarantee is most of what we offer students; the policy protects it.

## Always allowed (no permission needed)

1. **Truthful reference.** "We use Texture's Biology text." "A fork of
   Texture's Algebra I." "Compare Texture to commercial offerings."
2. **Unmodified redistribution.** Hosting, mirroring, printing, or selling
   verbatim copies of a published edition, marks intact, with the required
   CC BY attribution.
3. **Compatibility statements.** "Built with txt," "reads `.tx` files,"
   "imports Texture books" — for tools that actually do.
4. **Commentary, criticism, news, scholarship.**

## Requires written permission

- Using the marks in a product, service, event, or organization **name**
  or **domain name** (e.g., "TextureTutoring.com", "Texture Con").
- Merchandise bearing the marks.
- Co-branding or partnership language ("in partnership with Texture").

## Never allowed

- **Marks on modified content.** An adapted or partial edition may not be
  titled or branded as Texture's. Say "adapted from Texture's Algebra I,
  Edition 2027.1" (attribution — required); do not say "Texture Algebra I,
  Revised" (branding — prohibited).
- Implying Texture endorsement, review, or publication of anything Texture
  did not publish.
- Confusingly similar names, marks, or trade dress (including for forks of
  the txt software — fork freely, rename the fork).
- Use suggesting Texture endorses a political candidate, party, or cause.

## Fork naming guidance

| Intent | Do | Don't |
|--------|----|----|
| State adaptation | "Georgia Biology, adapted from Texture Biology" | "Texture Biology — Georgia Edition" |
| Translation awaiting review | "Biología (community translation of Texture's Biology)" | Texture logo on the cover |
| txt software fork | "weft (a fork of txt)" | "txt-pro", "txt2" |

Reviewed adaptations can earn official status — see
[governance §5](../community/governance.md) on upstream adoption and
"Texture Reviewed" designation.
