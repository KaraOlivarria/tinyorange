# Texture Contributor Agreement (Draft)

Version 0.1 (draft). **Not effective until reviewed by counsel.** Signed
once at account creation (electronic signature, versioned); re-signature
required only if terms materially change. Policy rationale — DCO-style
grant, no copyright assignment — is in [../licensing.md](../licensing.md).

---

## Part A — Certification of origin

By submitting a contribution to any Texture project, I certify that:

1. The contribution is my own original work, and I have the right to
   submit it under the licenses below; **or**
2. The contribution is based on prior work that, to the best of my
   knowledge, is covered under a compatible open license, and I have the
   right to submit that work with or without modifications, and I have
   identified that prior work and its license in the contribution's
   metadata; **or**
3. The contribution was provided to me by someone who certified 1 or 2,
   and I have not modified it.

I understand the contribution and its metadata (including my attribution
name and the submission record) are public and retained indefinitely as
part of the project's history.

## Part B — License grant

I retain copyright in my contribution. I grant to Texture and to all
recipients a perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, non-exclusive, royalty-free
license to my contribution:

- **Content contributions** (text, exercises, figures, translations, and
  similar): under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
  (CC BY 4.0)**.
- **Software contributions:** under the license of the receiving project —
  **Apache-2.0** (txt and related tooling) or **AGPL-3.0** (the Texture
  platform) — including, for Apache-2.0 projects, the patent grant in
  Apache-2.0 §3.

No copyright assignment occurs. Texture may not relicense my contribution
under terms other than these open licenses (later versions of the same
licenses, as published by Creative Commons or the license steward,
excepted).

## Part C — Attribution

Attribution name for the contributor record (real name required for
editorial roles per [collaborators.md §2](collaborators.md)):

> Name: ______________  ·  Optional: affiliation, ORCID

I understand attribution follows the [credit policy](collaborators.md), and
that I may request my name be changed in future builds (git history is
immutable).

## Part D — Contributors under 18

Contributors under 18 require a parent or guardian to co-sign this
agreement. Editorial roles require contributors to be 18 or older.

## Part E — Entity addendum (institutions)

Where contributions are made in the course of employment and the employer
holds rights (work-for-hire), an authorized representative of the employer
signs the **Entity Contributor Agreement** (same grants, made by the
entity, listing authorized contributors). Schools, districts, and
universities: one signature covers all listed staff.
